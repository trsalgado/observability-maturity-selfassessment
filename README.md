# Observability Maturity Self-Assessment · Autoavaliação de Observabilidade, AIOps & FinOps

## What it covers

26 dimensions in four pillars, each scored 1–5 from *Initial / Ad-hoc* to *Optimized / Predictive (AIOps)*:

| Pillar | Dimensions |
|---|---|
| **Observability fundamentals** (8) | Metrics and infrastructure coverage · Centralised, structured logs · Distributed tracing / APM · Instrumentation coverage (app, network, RUM, synthetics) · Signal unification (MELT) · Dashboards · Alerting and event management · SLO / SLI / error budgets |
| **AIOps** (7) | Anomaly detection · Correlation and noise reduction · Assisted root-cause analysis · Automation and auto-remediation · Prediction and capacity forecasting · ITSM and incident integration · Data quality and model governance |
| **Observability cost & FinOps** (6) | Cost visibility (showback/chargeback) · Ingestion optimisation and sampling · Retention and tiering · Cardinality and volume governance · Licensing and contracts · ROI and business value |
| **Process, people & governance** (5) | SRE culture and on-call · Skills and enablement · Standards and ownership (tags, naming) · Incident management and postmortems · Platform and tooling strategy |

## What you get

- **Level guide** describing what each score looks like in practice, dimension by dimension.
- **Results & radars** — your profile per pillar and an overall maturity level, generated automatically.
- **Quick TCO view** — a first order of magnitude for your annual observability cost (logs, metrics, traces, hosts and people).

## How to use

1. Download [`Autoavaliacao_Observabilidade_Maturidade_FinOps_SOFT_3.xlsx`](Autoavaliacao_Observabilidade_Maturidade_FinOps_SOFT_3.xlsx) (in Portuguese).
